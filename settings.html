Bomboclat

Your desk cat. Changes save as you make them.

Appearance

Mirror the cat to face the other way.

Behaviour

Keep the cat above other windows.

Clicks pass through the cat. It can't be dragged until you turn this off here or from the tray.

Turn almost invisible while the cursor is over it. Pairs well with click-through.

Privacy

Shows every key you type above the cat, passwords included. Leave off when sharing your screen.

System

Start Bomboclat when you sign in to Windows.

Show / hide cat CtrlShiftAltC
